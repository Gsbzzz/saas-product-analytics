# SaaS Product Analytics & Retention Intelligence

An end-to-end SaaS product analytics project focused on customer retention, monetization, engagement, acquisition quality, and customer success analytics.

This project simulates how modern SaaS companies use product analytics and business intelligence workflows to improve retention, optimize growth, and reduce churn.

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes key SaaS business and product metrics using SQL and Tableau to identify behavioral and operational patterns affecting:

- Customer retention
- Product engagement
- Monetization
- Acquisition quality
- Support efficiency

The analysis was performed on a SaaS subscription and churn dataset to simulate real-world product analytics workflows used in SaaS companies.

---

## Tools Used

- SQL
- Tableau
- CSV Datasets
- Product Analytics Frameworks

---

## Business Questions Explored

### 1. Which subscription plans experience the highest churn?
Analyzed churn patterns across Basic, Pro, and Enterprise plans.

### 2. Do annual subscribers retain better than monthly subscribers?
Compared retention performance between monthly and annual billing models.

### 3. Which acquisition channels bring high-retention customers?
Evaluated retention quality across organic, ads, partner, and event acquisition channels.

### 4. Which product features correlate with stronger retention?
Studied feature engagement behavior associated with lower churn rates.

### 5. Does support efficiency impact retention?
Analyzed how support resolution time influences customer retention outcomes.

---

## Key Insights

- Annual billing users retained significantly better than monthly subscribers.
- Certain product features strongly correlated with lower churn.
- Acquisition quality mattered more than acquisition volume.
- Longer support resolution times aligned with weaker retention outcomes.
- Retention patterns varied significantly across pricing tiers.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- Improve onboarding around high-retention features
- Push annual subscription incentives
- Prioritize high-retention acquisition channels
- Reduce support response and resolution time
- Use feature engagement as an early churn indicator
- Optimize pricing tier positioning
